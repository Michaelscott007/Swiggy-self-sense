# ShelfSense — Product Brief

## User
Instamart demand planner / procurement operator.

## Problem
Forecast systems predict demand. Planners still have to decide how much to actually order.

## Product
ShelfSense turns demand uncertainty and operating constraints into an explainable reorder recommendation.

## AI boundary
AI can help explain anomalies, summarise signals and generate scenarios.
Deterministic logic should remain the source of truth for inventory arithmetic, policy constraints and approved PO quantities.

## First experiment
Pilot on high-velocity perishables and shelf-stable products.

Measure fill rate, stockout minutes, waste/expiry, inventory days, override rate and planner time per SKU.
